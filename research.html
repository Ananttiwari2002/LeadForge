HR assignment / Research & analysis

Good research starts
with honest questions.

A proposed study for the HR professional app assignment: review current products, hear from HR app users, and test assumptions before locking the feature set.

Methodology proposalQuestionnaire preview onlyNo responses collected

Study sequence

Separate what is known
from what needs testing.

Current product claims and HR workflow assumptions should be checked against dated sources and participant input before becoming requirements.

  1. 01
    Desk researchReview recent HR technology writing and vendor documentation. Record publication date, source, and whether each point is a trend claim or a verified product feature.
  2. 02
    Neutral product comparisonApply the same workflow questions to Workday, BambooHR, and ADP Workforce Now. Compare by use case and evidence, not a universal winner.
  3. 03
    Questionnaire and interviewsRecruit relevant HR professionals and employees, pilot question wording, explain participation, and gather only data needed for the assignment.
  4. 04
    Synthesize and reviseSeparate observations from interpretation. Compare role-specific needs, note sample limitations, and revisit feature priorities.

Trends to verify

Working hypotheses,
not finished findings.

These themes are useful prompts for desk research, not claims that this assignment has confirmed:

  • More employee tasks are moving to mobile and self-service experiences.
  • HR platforms increasingly connect workflow data across payroll, talent, and workforce operations.
  • Automation and AI are being explored for routine HR tasks, with oversight and data quality still central concerns.
  • People analytics can inform planning, but access, context, and small-group privacy affect responsible use.
  • Hybrid work raises the importance of clear, timely, accessible communication.

For each theme, capture a dated source, setting, limitations, and whether it applies to the selected organization and participant group.

Comparison framework

Compare the work,
not a leaderboard.

These are established products in the HR software market. The framing below is a starting lens only; validate current capabilities using official product materials and hands-on evaluation.

Product to examine

Workday

Explore how its HR workflows, employee experience, configuration, reporting, and connected enterprise systems fit the intended organizational context.

Questions: Which tasks are straightforward for employees? What setup and role administration are needed?

Product to examine

BambooHR

Examine how common people-management and employee-facing workflows are presented, and which organization sizes or operating needs they support.

Questions: What is included in the chosen plan? How do data access and integrations work?

Product to examine

ADP Workforce Now

Study how HR administration, payroll-related processes, employee access, and reporting connect for the organization's requirements.

Questions: Which workflows depend on configuration, region, or additional services?

No comparative testing or ranking has been conducted for this prototype. Product packaging, features, and availability may change; cite dated sources in the completed assignment.

Questionnaire preview

Built from the
course brief.

Preview adapted from the supplied HRMM504 feedback questionnaire. It is not interactive and does not collect responses.

Before field useThe supplied draft asks for an organization name and role information. Review consent, anonymity, data minimization, storage, and course ethics requirements before circulating it.
  1. Role in the organization HR manager / employee / administrator / other
  2. Frequency of HR app use Daily / weekly / monthly / rarely
  3. Which HR apps are currently used? Workday / BambooHR / ADP Workforce Now / other
  4. Which HR functions are primarily supported? Recruitment and onboarding / performance / payroll and benefits / all listed
  5. Usability and visual clarity Navigation, layout, labels, and ease of use for non-technical users
  6. Reliability and guidance Performance, instructions, support options, and peak usage
  7. Workflow coverage and integration Task completion, essential features, connected systems, and integration quality
  8. Communication and self-service Notifications, HR-to-employee communication, leave and payroll access
  9. Confidence and mobile experience Security confidence, mobile functionality, and data protection
  10. Overall value and satisfaction Manual effort, reporting usefulness, recommendation, and needs met

For the attitude statements (items 5-26 in the source), the assignment provides a five-point Strongly agree to Strongly disagree scale. Negative statements, such as performance issues, should be coded carefully.
