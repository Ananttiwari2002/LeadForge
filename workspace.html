How the score is built

No black box.
Just arithmetic you can read.

Every score is a weighted sum of five named factors. Move a weight and the ranking responds immediately, so reviewers can see exactly which signal is driving the order.

01

Sector match

How closely the organization's sector aligns with the profile you defined as the target.

02

Company size

Whether headcount lands inside the band you consider a realistic fit for the offer.

03

Region fit

Geography relative to the markets you can serve well today, not everywhere at once.

04

Buying signal

Public, permitted signals such as a stated initiative, funding event, or expansion note.

05

Source reliability

How well-sourced and recent the underlying record is, so confident claims stay confident.

06

Human triage

Move a row through new, review, shortlist, or pass. The pipeline state is yours to own.

Guardrails

Useful without
being careless.

This workspace is deliberately conservative. It demonstrates the review layer that should sit between a search and any real outreach.

Data

All twelve organizations are invented for demonstration. No real company, person, or contact detail is shown or stored.

Storage

Your pipeline choices are saved only in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data resets everything.

Export

The CSV is generated on your device from the rows currently on screen. It never leaves your machine.
