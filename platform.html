Turn a wide search

A proposed AI-assisted workspace for discovering business prospects from permitted public information, applying transparent fit criteria, and keeping a person in the review loop.

A possible workflow

Show the work behind
each suggestion.

In a future implementation, every record would carry source, recency, and rationale. AI might help summarize source-grounded evidence or compare records with user-set criteria, but this static prototype has no model or functioning pipeline.

  1. DefineSet geography, sector, size, and exclusions.
  2. DiscoverUse sources approved for the intended purpose.
  3. NormalizeDeduplicate organization-level facts.
  4. QualifyApply explicit, editable criteria.
  5. ReviewCheck evidence, uncertainty, and fit.
  6. DecideRecord a human-owned next step.

Qualification rubric

Fit is a question
to inspect, not trust.

A score would organize evidence against chosen criteria, not predict business success. Reviewers must be able to see and challenge each factor.

Problem relevance

Is there a public, current signal related to the offer being explored?

Organization fit

Does the organization match the stated sector, size, and geography?

Evidence quality

Is the source attributable, recent enough, and appropriate to use?

Human context

What is missing, ambiguous, or inappropriate to infer automatically?

Important boundaryContact discovery, outreach, profiling of individuals, scraping, and AI-generated conclusions are not implemented here. Sample records contain no individual contact details. Any future assistance should cite permitted evidence, expose uncertainty, avoid unsupported inference, and require human review; source rights, privacy, and model validation must be assessed first.
