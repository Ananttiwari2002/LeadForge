This is a limited explanatory note for a student frontend prototype, not a contract for an operating service.

Concept note / November 2026

Not a launch-ready legal documentBefore public or commercial launch, the responsible organization must obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review and publish terms that accurately match its services, data practices, and obligations.

Academic concept

LeadForge is a design and course-project showcase. The lead intelligence direction and HR people operations assignment are distinct concepts. The site does not offer an operational product, professional service, data service, or account.

Illustrative content

All sample organizations, fit scores, workflow states, personas, priorities, and proposed system details are provided to explain possible interface behavior. They should not be treated as real records, factual assessments, recommendations, or research findings.

No completed research or live automation

The showcase does not claim that HR survey feedback has been collected, that a comparative product evaluation is complete, or that current market trends have been verified. It does not conduct web scraping, lead enrichment, automated qualification, employment decision-making, or outreach.

Local interactions

Page navigation and sample filtering run in your browser. The contact form checks fields in the browser and displays a local-only confirmation; it does not transmit or store submissions. No account, backend, integration, AI, or persistence is provided.

Use and reliance

Content is for discussion and educational demonstration. Verify any information independently before using it to make a business, employment, legal, or other consequential decision. Nothing in this prototype is legal, HR, or business advice.

Changes and applicable terms

This note may be replaced if the prototype is developed further. A real deployment should identify its operator, applicable law, permitted uses, intellectual property terms, warranty and liability provisions, dispute process, and user support after qualified legal review.
