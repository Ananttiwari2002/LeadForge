Project notes / Privacy

A clear note on
this prototype.

This page describes the current academic showcase only. It is not a privacy notice for an operating product or organization.

Concept note / November 2026

Not legal advice or a launch-ready policyAny real product would need a privacy assessment and jurisdiction-specific legal review before launch, including review of its hosting, vendors, data flows, and intended use.

What this site does

This is a static frontend concept. It does not provide accounts, connect to a backend, run analytics, collect lead information, process HR records, or use an AI model.

Feedback form

The form on the feedback page demonstrates browser-native validation and a local confirmation. It does not transmit or persist values. The confirmation clears the fields. With JavaScript disabled, the control is inactive and no information is submitted. Please do not enter sensitive, personal, employee, or confidential information.

Browser storage and cookies

The frontend does not intentionally set cookies, use local storage, or create a tracking identifier. Your browser and the environment serving these static files may independently keep ordinary technical records; those hosting and browser behaviors are outside this frontend and should be documented by whoever deploys it.

Third-party services

The pages use local CSS and JavaScript files, system fonts, and no embedded external image, analytics, or font service. A future deployment must review its hosting provider, security logs, and any added services separately.

Future product concepts

The lead intelligence and HR app pages are separate proposals, not active services. No actual prospect or employee data is processed by this showcase. If either concept were developed, its purpose, lawful basis where applicable, access model, data minimization, retention, correction, deletion, and incident processes would need to be designed before use.

Contact and changes

There is no inbox or privacy contact service attached to this concept site. A deployer should replace this section with an accountable contact and revise this notice whenever real processing or third-party services are introduced.
