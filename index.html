The challenge, answered

Scattered effort in.
A reviewable shortlist out.

The problem statement named five practical frictions in finding and qualifying clients. Here is how each one could be answered without adding new risk.

Searching many platforms by hand to find relevant opportunities.

→

One governed search surfaceDescribe the target profile once — sector, region, size, exclusions — and let an approved source set do the broad sweep.

Collecting business details from scattered, inconsistent places.

→

Organization-level normalizationRecords are de-duplicated and kept to permitted, attributable public facts — with the source shown beside every claim.

Judging which prospects actually fit the business requirement.

→

Transparent weighted scoringFive named factors re-rank the list as you weight them, so the reason behind every score is visible, never a black box.

Handing business development something they can actually use.

→

A structured, exportable baseSort, triage through pipeline states, and export a clean CSV — the qualified lead database the team asked for.

Losing hours to repetitive research and update work.

→

Freed-up human judgementManual effort drops so people spend their time on the conversations, not the collecting — the efficiency the study set out to explore.

A calmer kind of research

From scattered sources
to a useful next step.

LeadForge sketches how public business signals might be organized into a reviewable workspace. People stay in control of what counts as a fit and what happens next.

Business development often starts with a broad question and a dozen browser tabs. This concept explores a more legible path from a target profile to a short list worth checking.

It is a proposal, not an AI system or a data-collection service. The interface uses fictional sample records to make workflow decisions concrete.

Walk through the concept →
  1. 01
    Set a directionDescribe a market, organization profile, or signal to investigate.
  2. 02
    Collect with carePotential public sources would be checked for access rights, relevance, and freshness.
  3. 03
    Review the evidenceSee why a record surfaced, where it came from, and what still needs verification.

Expected outcome

Less busywork.
More deliberate decisions.

The study set an objective rather than a promise. These are the outcomes the concept is designed to move, and the guardrails that keep it honest.

Workload

Reduce manual research

Replace hours of tab-hopping with a single, filterable surface for the same facts.

Productivity

Protect skilled time

Let sales and business development spend their attention on judgement, not collection.

Business development

Sharpen targeting

Weighted criteria make the notion of a “good fit” explicit, shareable, and reviewable.

Acquisition

Faster, cleaner pipeline

A structured export moves qualified leads into the next step without re-keying.
