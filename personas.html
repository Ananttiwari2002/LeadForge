Design for the person
behind the task.

Four provisional personas help make the concepts specific. They are synthesized assumptions for discussion, not research participants or evidence of validated user needs.

Provisional personasValidate with intended usersTwo separate product contexts
Track 01 / Lead intelligence

Business-development lead

Context: Responsible for identifying organizations that might benefit from a service, with limited time for broad research.

Goal: Find relevant prospects and understand why they appear on a shortlist.

Friction: Information is scattered, source quality varies, and a score without evidence is hard to trust.

Workflow: Define a target profile, review signals and original sources, correct weak matches, and choose whether to pursue a next step.

"Show me what makes this worth a closer look."

Track 02 / HR assignment

HR manager

Context: Coordinates people processes across managers and employees, potentially using multiple systems.

Goal: Keep onboarding, attendance, benefits, and employee questions moving with clear ownership.

Friction: Repeated data entry, unclear status, scattered guidance, and limited time to interpret reports.

Workflow: Check work in progress, resolve an exception, share accurate guidance, and follow up with the right person.

"Help me see what is waiting and who needs me."

Track 02 / HR assignment

Employee

Context: Needs to complete occasional HR tasks while balancing their main role, often on a phone or between shifts.

Goal: Find trusted information, submit a request, and know what happens next.

Friction: Hard-to-find policies, unclear request status, and jargon that makes a simple task feel uncertain.

Workflow: Check a benefit or policy, send a leave or profile request, receive an update, and find support if needed.

"Let me finish the task without guessing."

Track 02 / HR assignment

System administrator / recruiter

Context: Keeps people records, role access, or recruiting workflows organized. This combined assignment persona needs refinement because these jobs may differ substantially.

Goal: Maintain accurate records and move candidates or employee changes through a controlled workflow.

Friction: Duplicate entry, permissions that are difficult to reason about, and exceptions without a clear audit trail.

Workflow: Set up access or a requisition, track a change, resolve a mismatch, and confirm the correct information reached the right user.

"Make the exception visible, and the fix traceable."

Next validation step

Replace assumptions
with real context.

For the HR assignment, recruit representative HR professionals and employees, test the survey wording, and ask participants to describe recent workflows rather than speculate about abstract feature lists.

For the lead concept, interview business-development practitioners about source trust, qualification, record freshness, and the appropriate boundary between research assistance and outreach.
