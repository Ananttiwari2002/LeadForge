Track 02 / HRMM504 course assignment

People work should
feel less scattered.

A separate HR professional app concept for connecting core employee workflows with clearer communication and self-service. Priorities below are provisional hypotheses, not findings from collected feedback.

Separate project trackNeeds validation with HR professionalsNo participant feedback collected yet

Assignment objective

One connected view
of people operations.

The HRMM504 assignment asks for research into HR technology, a neutral review of existing apps, feedback from HR professionals, defined user personas, and a prioritized feature set.

This showcase provides a proposed direction and study structure. It does not report completed interviews, survey results, or a finished product specification.

See the proposed research method →

Provisional feature map

Start with reliable
everyday essentials.

Priority reflects a design hypothesis: first make frequent, foundational work dependable; then add connected communication and decision support. Validate this order with users.

01

Employee onboarding

Coordinate a new hire's first steps, documents, equipment, and introductions with clear ownership and progress visibility.

P0
02

Employee self-service

Give employees a straightforward place to update permitted profile details, find policies, and track requests or key tasks.

P0
03

Time and attendance

Support time entry, attendance review, and correction requests with transparent status and an auditable approval path.

P0
04

Benefits access

Present plan information, eligibility guidance, and enrollment actions with clear links to authoritative benefit providers.

P0
05

Performance conversations

Help managers and employees prepare goals, check-ins, and review notes, with careful role permissions and employee visibility.

P1
06

Communication tools

Deliver targeted announcements and practical updates; avoid turning important HR messages into an unmanageable chat stream.

P1
07

Reporting and analytics

Offer aggregated operational views with definitions, access controls, and suppression for groups too small to report safely.

P1

P0 = foundational first release hypothesis. P1 = follow-on capability after workflows, permissions, and data quality are validated.

Design principles

Useful for people
doing real work.

One clear next action

Show status, owner, and next step rather than making users search across modules.

Respect role and context

Employees, HR, recruiters, and administrators need different views and access.

Keep a human path

High-impact decisions remain explainable, reviewable, and open to correction.

Make mobile practical

Prioritize quick checks and requests without shrinking complex workflows into tiny forms.

Research statusNo HR professionals have completed the provided questionnaire for this prototype. Feature priorities and user needs are working hypotheses to test, not survey findings.
