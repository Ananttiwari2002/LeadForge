Why this order

Foundations first,
then connections.

The feature order follows the assignment brief: make frequent, foundational work dependable before layering on communication and decision support.

P0

Onboarding

First impressions are set in week one; a clear checklist removes the most common new-starter friction.

P0

Self-service

Most questions are simple status checks. Answering them in-app removes the bulk of routine HR email.

P0

Time & attendance

Payroll depends on accurate, auditable time records — a foundational data-quality requirement.

P0

Benefits

Presenting plans clearly, with enrolment links, reduces confusion at the yearly window.

P1

Performance

Valuable, but only once permissions and record-keeping are trustworthy.

P1

Communication

Targeted announcements need the workflow foundations above them to stay signal, not noise.

P1

Reporting

Analytics demand clean data and careful suppression before they can be shown safely.

Prototype statusThis is a design demonstration, not a working HR system. No real employee data is used, nothing is stored on a server, and feature priorities remain hypotheses to validate with HR professionals.
