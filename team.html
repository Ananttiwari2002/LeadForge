Project timeline

How the work
moved forward.

The rail fills as you scroll, and each milestone slides into place in turn. Dates marked as allotted or submitted come from the HRMM504 brief.

  1. 06 Nov 2026

    Brief allotted

    The group task is issued: research HR technology, define target users, and propose a prioritised HR feature set — with a lead-generation problem statement added alongside it.

    • Scope and group roles agreed
    • Two problem statements separated
  2. Phase 01

    Research and analysis

    Desk research into HR technology trends, a neutral comparison frame for Workday, BambooHR and ADP Workforce Now, and a 26-item feedback questionnaire for HR professionals.

    • Trends logged as hypotheses
    • Questionnaire drafted, not yet fielded
  3. Phase 02

    Target audience

    Four provisional personas covering business development, the HR manager, the employee, and an administrator or recruiter — each with goals, friction, and a workflow.

    • Personas marked provisional
    • Validation step defined
  4. Phase 03

    Feature set and interface

    Seven core HR capabilities prioritised P0 and P1, then shaped into a responsive interface with onboarding, self-service, attendance, benefits, performance, communication and reporting.

    • Priority tied to user needs
    • Design principles documented
  5. Phase 04

    Lead intelligence build

    The AI lead-generation statement becomes a working front-end: a weighted qualification model, a filterable workspace, pipeline states, and CSV export of qualified leads.

    • Scoring factors stay inspectable
    • Data layer swappable for a real API
  6. 21 Nov 2026

    Submission

    The showcase is packaged for the group submission: research approach, personas, prioritised features, feasibility and risk, and a documented prototype.

    • Static build, no backend
    • Sample data clearly labelled

Disciplines

What a build like this
actually requires.

Four disciplines carried this project from brief to prototype. They are listed as the work itself, not as claims about individual members.

01Research

Evidence and analysis

Trend review, product comparison framing, questionnaire design, and separating observation from interpretation.

02Product

Interface and structure

Information architecture, responsive layout, accessibility, and the wording that keeps an AI concept honest.

03Engineering

Front-end build

Scoring model, filtering, pipeline persistence, CSV export, and a data layer ready to swap for a real API.

04Delivery

Documentation

Feasibility, risk controls, legal notes, and packaging the group submission for assessment.
